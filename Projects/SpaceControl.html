I had the opportunity to work on Space Control as part of my internship at MonnMonster Studios (link). During my internship I would work on various tasks ranging from fixing bugs to implementing new features.

What I did

Here's a quick overview of some of the work I did during my internship, being a Gameplay Programmer my tasks varied a lot:

  • Polishing mechanics to AAA quality
  • Adding Hand Tracking to a quality far beyond what the current market offered
  • In-game cosmetics dispenser for hats, glasses and hands
  • Cosmetics persistence to the game
  • Infinite Wave GameMode
  • Physics and Rendering Optimizations
  • Various Audio Implementation
  • Various Instruments
  • Fidget toys
  • Polishing the game's climax for optimal player feel
  • Animations and Visual Effects
  • Special Powers for Characters affecting gravity
  • Various interactive UI elements and dispensers
  • Playtesting and Iteration
  • Cutting Voicelines
  • Many, many, many bug fixes

I learned a lot during my internship, and quite happy that I could meaningfully contribute to such an exciting project! Special thanks to the the team, who would later become colleagues, for teaching me and letting me grow with the company.
I learned many valuable skills and gained valuable practical experience in game development, particularly in working efficiently and effectively under pressure, tight deadlines and with legacy code.

Be on the lookout for more updates in September with the new turret GameMode and Hand Tracking Update!
You can read more about all of my work on my complete internship report. As well as find out more about the project on Meta!
